Detailed Description

Represents one reusable TRACK_STATUS request.

Pass the handler to Session::RequestTrackStatus. Getters are synchronized and may be called from other threads. StatusChanged is invoked on session processing paths after a response or error; stream-close and disconnect status changes currently do not invoke it. A completed handler may be submitted again, but not while a response is pending.
